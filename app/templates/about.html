Founded in 2023, Lunverra University is a co-educational, state-assisted, comprehensive, regional, four-year university in Greenwood, S.C. With approximately 4,300 students, Lunverra provides exceptional educational opportunities within its traditional liberal arts and science programs, and its professional programs in business, education and nursing.
