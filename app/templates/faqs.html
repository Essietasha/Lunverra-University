FAQs

General feedback and questions about Bates College can be shared with us through this form.

If you would like to study at Bates or are looking for scholarship or financial aid information, view our Admissions page or Financial Services page. To reach a specific Bates office or department, you may want to start with our listing of academic departments/programs, offices, and sports teams, or our A-Z Index.

How do I apply to the university?

You can apply online through our official application portal or via common platforms like the Common App or UCAS (if applicable). Check our Admissions page for deadlines, required documents, and application steps.

Is financial aid or scholarships available?

The Department of Campus Safety is 24 hour, 365-day service oriented department with the mission of providing a safe and secure environment for all those on our campus, and leading by example, through education, and enforcement.

Can international students apply?

Absolutely. We welcome students from around the world. International applicants should refer to the International Students section for visa information, English language requirements, and application procedures.

What is campus life like?

Campus life is vibrant and diverse with over 100 student clubs, cultural events, sports, and student-led initiatives. Our Student Life page has more details on housing, dining, and extracurriculars.

What support services are available for students?

We provide academic advising, career counseling, mental health support, accessibility services, and tutoring. Our goal is to help you thrive both academically and personally.

How do I schedule a campus visit or virtual tour?

You can book a guided campus tour or attend a virtual information session through our Visit Us page. We encourage prospective students to explore the campus before applying.
