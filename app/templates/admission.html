Eligibility Requirements

To be considered for admission into any programme at Lunverra University, applicants must meet the following:

🎓 For Undergraduate Applicants (Home and International)

1. Academic Qualifications

Home Applicants
  • A minimum of three A-Levels with grades dependent on the course applied for (typically ABB–BBC).
  • Some courses may accept BTEC Level 3 Extended Diploma or T Levels in relevant subjects.
International Applicants
  • Equivalent qualifications to A-Levels (e.g. High School Diploma, IB, Indian HSC, etc.).
  • Must include strong passes in relevant subjects, including English and Mathematics.

2. Academic Qualifications

Minimum of 5 GCSEs at grades 9–4 (A–C)* including English Language and Mathematics.

3. English Language Proficiency

For non-native English speakers:

  • IELTS Academic: Minimum overall band of 6.0–6.5 (depending on the programme).
  • Other accepted tests: TOEFL iBT, Cambridge English, or equivalent.

4. Personal Statement

A well-written UCAS personal statement explaining:

  • Motivation for the course
  • Relevant experience or activities
  • At least one academic reference is required, submitted via UCAS or during direct application.

🎓 For Postgraduate (Master’s) Applicants

1. Academic Requirements

  • A Bachelor's degree (2:2 or above) or international equivalent.
  • Specific programmes may require a 2:1 or higher in a related subject.

2. English Language Proficiency

  • IELTS Academic: Typically 6.5 overall, with no component less than 6.0.
  • Proof of medium of instruction in English may be accepted for some applicants.

3. Work Experience (if applicable)

Some programmes may require relevant professional or research experience.

4. References & Personal Statement

  • Two references (at least one academic)
  • A personal statement outlining your motivation and suitability
